# BANKING77 data attribution

This repository includes the author-released `train.csv` and `test.csv` files from:

- Casanueva, I., Temcinas, T., Gerz, D., Henderson, M., & Vulić, I. (2020). *Efficient Intent Detection with Dual Sentence Encoders*. ACL Workshop on NLP for Conversational AI.
- Source repository: https://github.com/PolyAI-LDN/task-specific-datasets
- Source commit: `57ec275d8078af65b7731c2a98be812d844a6d6b`
- Dataset page: https://huggingface.co/datasets/PolyAI/banking77

The BANKING77 dataset is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The dataset files are included unchanged. Their SHA-256 hashes are recorded and verified by `ticketroute/data.py`.
